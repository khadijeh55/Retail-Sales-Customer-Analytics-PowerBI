# Power BI Dashboard Overview

This folder is designated for the retail analytics Power BI dashboard (`.pbix`) and its visual previews.

## 📊 Key Dashboard Features:
* **Executive Summary:** High-level KPIs including Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), and Total Customers.
* **Customer Segmentation Matrix:** Visualizing RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) scores to highlight VIP customers vs. at-risk customers.
* **Sales Trends & Seasonality:** Line charts tracking revenue growth across months and peak shopping hours.
* **Product Category Performance:** Bar charts breaking down sales and profit margins by product categories.
